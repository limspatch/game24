# Privacy Policy for 24 Game

**Effective Date: September 21, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how 24 Game ("the App") handles user information.

## 1. Information We Collect

24 Game does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Location
- Contacts
- Photos or media
- Device identifiers
- Payment information

## 2. Data Stored on Your Device

24 Game may store game-related information locally on your device, such as:

- Game progress
- Best scores and records
- Game statistics
- Settings and preferences

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to or accessible by the developer.

## 3. Internet and Network Access

24 Game is designed to function without requiring an internet connection.

The App does not operate a backend server and does not transmit game data or personal information to external servers.

## 4. Third-Party Services

24 Game does not currently use third-party services that collect or process users' personal information.

The App does not currently include:

- Advertising services
- Analytics services
- Social media integrations
- User account services
- Cloud data synchronization

If third-party services are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 5. Children's Privacy

24 Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Because the App does not collect personal information, users can play the game without providing personal information to the developer.

## 6. Data Security

Because 24 Game does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal information stored on the developer's servers.

Game data stored locally on the device is subject to the security of the user's device and operating system.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the App or its data practices.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
